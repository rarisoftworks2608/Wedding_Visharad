The Story

Sonal Jagtap’s Wedding Four Celebrations, One Story.

Four celebrations, each with its own mood. A sunflower-and-marigold haldi in a rose garden, a colourful mehendi, a garden sangeet under the lights and a traditional wedding framed by red roses and carved jaali arches.

  1. I.Haldi in the Rose Garden
  2. II.A Colourful Mehendi
  3. III.A Garden Sangeet
  4. IV.The Wedding
I.
Chapter I

Haldi in the Rose Garden

Sunflower arches, marigold strings and a chevron walkway set the tone for a bright, joyful morning with family.

II.
Chapter II

A Colourful Mehendi

Dreamcatchers, tassels and a garden lounge for an afternoon of music, colour and mehendi.

III.
Chapter III

A Garden Sangeet

A green floral tunnel, mirror balls and a lit dance floor for a night of performances under the open sky.

IV.
Chapter IV

The Wedding

Red-and-white rose walls, carved jaali arches and a petal-strewn aisle leading to the pheras.
