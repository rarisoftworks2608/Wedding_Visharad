Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan destination weddings outside Pune?

Yes. We plan and execute weddings across India — including Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa, Kerala and Lonavala — as well as international destinations, handling venue scouting, travel, logistics and local vendors.

Can we book a single service, such as décor or hospitality?

Absolutely. You can choose complete wedding planning or individual services like décor & styling, guest hospitality, photography or event production — whatever your celebration needs.

Do you manage multi-day celebrations like haldi, mehendi and sangeet?

Yes. We design and coordinate every chapter of a multi-day wedding — from haldi and mehendi to sangeet, the wedding ceremony and the reception — with one dedicated team.

How are your wedding packages priced?

Every wedding is different, so we create a custom proposal based on your guest count, venue, number of events and the services you need. Share a few details through our enquiry form and we’ll get back within 24 hours.

How early should we start planning with you?

The earlier the better, especially for peak wedding season and destination weddings — it gives more choice of venues and dates. That said, reach out whatever your timeline and we’ll tell you what’s possible.
